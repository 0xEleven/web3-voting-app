# PRODUCT.md — School Decentralized Voting dApp

## 1. Product Truth & Mission
A verifiable, decentralized student election platform built on Ethereum smart contracts. Designed as a school capstone project to demonstrate the complete 4-tier Web3 architecture: Frontend (React) → Web3/Wallet (MetaMask/Ethers v6) → Smart Contracts (Solidity 0.8.20) → Blockchain Ledger (Hardhat 31337).

## 3. Audience & Context
- **Audience:** Academic evaluators, computer science faculty, student voters.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, civic, transparent, calm, trustworthy.
- **Operating Context:** Local blockchain environment (Hardhat Localhost RPC 8545), responsive on desktop and mobile.

## 4. Visual Philosophy & Design Laws (Impeccable Principles)
- **No AI Slop:** Strictly eliminate purple-to-blue gradients, gradient text, zero-offset neon glow shadows, side-tab stripes, and card-in-card nesting.
- **Color Discipline:** Muted slate obsidian neutral palette with solid civic emerald/cobalt accents. Solid, readable button fills meeting WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum contrast.
- **Distinctive Typography:** `Epilogue` for authoritative institutional headings, `Albert Sans` for crisp UI body, `IBM Plex Mono` for contract hashes and addresses.
- **Layout & Structure:** Tactile ballot sheets, integrated ledger audit trail, inline architectural disclosures instead of disruptive modal overlays.
- **Accessibility:** 65-75 character max line length for sustained reading comfort, clear focus rings, semantic markup.
